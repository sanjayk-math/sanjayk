About

Ph.D. in Mathematics, The Gandhigram Rural Institute (Deemed to be University)

I completed my Ph.D. viva voce in Mathematics under Dr. P. Balasubramaniam, Senior Professor of Mathematics. My thesis, Stabilization and Synchronization of Nonlinear Dynamical Systems using Hybrid Quantum Algorithm, builds control methods for nonlinear and uncertain systems.

The work combines Lyapunov stability theory, linear matrix inequality optimization, quantum genetic algorithms and physics-informed neural networks. It targets stabilization, synchronization and filtering in interconnected, large-scale systems affected by time delays, memory effects, cyber-attacks, uncertainty and fractional-order dynamics.

Research interests

  • Nonlinear dynamical systems
  • Quantum computing
  • Control systems
  • Image processing
